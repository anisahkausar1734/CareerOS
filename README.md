# CareerOS

CareerOS is an AI-powered career intelligence platform that helps students identify skill gaps, build personalized learning roadmaps, improve resume quality, and track career readiness through data-driven insights.

The platform is designed to serve as a unified career operating system, enabling students to transform career goals into structured, measurable development plans.

---

## Overview

The modern learning ecosystem provides unlimited access to courses, tutorials, certifications, and career resources. However, many students struggle to determine which skills are actually relevant to their target roles and how to systematically prepare for internships and full-time opportunities.

CareerOS addresses this challenge by combining career planning, skill assessment, resume intelligence, and progress tracking into a single platform.

---

## Problem Statement

Students often face several challenges during their career preparation journey:

* Lack of clarity regarding skills required for specific roles
* Difficulty identifying gaps between current abilities and industry expectations
* Dependence on generic learning roadmaps that do not align with individual goals
* Limited understanding of resume quality and ATS requirements
* Absence of a structured system to measure career readiness and progress

As a result, students frequently spend time learning technologies without a clear strategy or measurable outcomes.

---

## Solution

CareerOS provides a personalized, data-driven approach to career development.

The platform evaluates a student's profile, compares existing skills against industry requirements, identifies improvement areas, and generates actionable recommendations that guide continuous growth.

By consolidating career planning, skill development, and progress management into a single system, CareerOS helps students make informed decisions and prepare more effectively for professional opportunities.

---

## Core Features

### Career Profile Management

Create and maintain a comprehensive professional profile containing skills, interests, educational background, and career objectives.

### Skill Gap Analysis

Compare existing competencies against target job requirements and identify high-priority skills that require development.

### Personalized Learning Roadmaps

Generate structured learning paths with milestones, recommended topics, and progress checkpoints tailored to individual career goals.

### Resume Intelligence

Analyze resumes for skill relevance, content quality, and ATS compatibility while providing actionable improvement suggestions.

### Career Readiness Dashboard

Monitor overall progress through career readiness metrics, skill development tracking, and roadmap completion analytics.

### AI-Powered Recommendations

Receive personalized suggestions for skills, learning resources, certifications, and development activities based on career objectives.
