# Core Features

## 1. Career Intelligence Engine

Understand where you stand today and where you need to go.

CareerOS analyzes a student's current skills and compares them against the requirements of their target role to provide actionable career insights.

### Capabilities

* Career Goal Selection
* Skill Assessment
* Career Readiness Scoring
* Skill Gap Identification
* Growth Recommendations

---

## 2. Personalized Learning Roadmaps

Turn career goals into structured action plans.

Based on identified skill gaps, CareerOS generates personalized learning paths that help students focus on the right skills at the right time.

### Capabilities

* Goal-Based Roadmaps
* Learning Milestones
* Progress Tracking
* Recommended Learning Resources
* Skill Development Plans

---

## 3. Resume Intelligence

Build resumes that effectively communicate skills and potential.

CareerOS evaluates resumes and provides insights to improve quality, relevance, and ATS compatibility.

### Capabilities

* Resume Upload & Analysis
* Skill Extraction
* ATS Readiness Assessment
* Resume Improvement Suggestions
* Skill Coverage Analysis

---

## 4. Career Readiness Dashboard

Track progress through measurable career metrics.

The dashboard provides a centralized view of learning progress, skill growth, and overall career preparedness.

### Capabilities

* Readiness Score
* Skill Progress Monitoring
* Roadmap Completion Tracking
* Career Insights
* Growth Analytics

---

## 5. AI-Powered Guidance

Receive intelligent recommendations tailored to individual goals.

CareerOS leverages AI to help students make informed decisions throughout their career development journey.

### Capabilities

* Personalized Recommendations
* Learning Suggestions
* Career Insights
* Skill Prioritization
* Development Guidance

---

# Why CareerOS?

Most platforms help students learn.

CareerOS helps students answer three critical questions:

### Where am I today?

Through career readiness assessments and skill analysis.

### What am I missing?

Through intelligent skill gap identification.

### What should I do next?

Through personalized roadmaps and AI-driven recommendations.

CareerOS transforms career preparation from guesswork into a structured, measurable process.
