# CareerOS Roadmap

## Vision

CareerOS aims to become the personal career operating system for students by helping them understand where they stand, what they need to learn, and how to systematically prepare for internships and professional opportunities.

Our goal is to transform career aspirations into clear, actionable, and measurable growth plans.

---

## Milestone 1 — Build the Foundation

Establish the core platform and create a seamless user experience for onboarding and profile management.

### Deliverables

* Project Architecture Setup
* React Frontend
* Spring Boot Backend
* MySQL Database Integration
* Secure Authentication (JWT)
* User Registration & Login
* Career Profile Management

### Outcome

Students can create an account, build their profile, and securely access the platform.

---

## Milestone 2 — Unlock Career Intelligence

Enable students to understand their strengths, identify skill gaps, and receive structured guidance.

### Deliverables

* Career Goal Selection
* Skill Gap Analysis Engine
* Career Readiness Assessment
* Personalized Learning Roadmaps
* Progress Tracking System

### Outcome

Students receive a clear understanding of what skills they need and a personalized path to achieve their goals.

---

## Milestone 3 — Deliver Personalized Growth

Provide intelligent insights that help students improve their employability and career readiness.

### Deliverables

* Resume Analysis & Evaluation
* ATS Compatibility Assessment
* Resume Improvement Recommendations
* AI-Powered Career Suggestions
* Career Dashboard & Analytics

### Outcome

Students can continuously improve their resumes, track their progress, and make data-driven career decisions.

---

## Future Vision

CareerOS will continue evolving into a comprehensive career development ecosystem with features such as:

* AI Career Assistant
* Mock Interview Platform
* Internship Recommendation Engine
* LinkedIn Profile Analysis
* Certification Tracking
* Mobile Application

---

## Current Focus

### MVP Development

The current focus is on building the platform foundation, implementing authentication, and establishing the architecture required for future career intelligence features.
